Overview

In one of the most dynamic and rapidly expanding areas of Jesolo, near Piazza Milano, we offer for sale a refined sea front three-room apartment, set in an elegant newly constructed complex with a swimming pool.

Upon entering, you are welcomed by a bright and well-organized living area where the living room and open kitchen merge into a unique and harmonious space. It is an environment designed for everyday life that naturally opens up to the outside: from here you can access a beautiful livable terrace, the ideal place to dine outdoors or simply relax while breathing in the sea air.

Continuing inside, the hallway leads to two bedrooms: the master bedroom has direct access to the livable terrace, while the smaller room perfect for children or friends features its own independent second terrace.

Completing the apartment are two full bathrooms, a rare and valuable convenience that ensures maximum practicality both for daily use and when hosting guests. A well-distributed modern home designed to offer maximum comfort in every detail.

Equipped with autonomous heating system and a valuable covered parking space owned by property, this property guarantees excellent comfort throughout all seasons. Features that make it perfect not only as a summer holiday home but also as an actual residence to live year-round.

Varcata la soglia, si viene accolti da una zona giorno luminosa e ben organizzata, dove soggiorno e cucina a vista si fondono in uno spazio unico e armonioso. A completare l'appartamento troviamo due bagni completi, una comodità rara e preziosa che garantisce la massima praticità sia nell'uso quotidiano sia quando si hanno ospiti. Una casa ben distribuita e moderna, pensata per offrire il massimo del comfort in ogni dettaglio.
