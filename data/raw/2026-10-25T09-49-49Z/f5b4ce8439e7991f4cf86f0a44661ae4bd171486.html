Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo!

Just a few steps from the renowned Piazza Mazzini, we offer for sale an elegant three-room apartment located within the prestigious residence Palms Edition. A perfect solution both as a primary residence and as a holiday home.

With an area of 82 sqm, the apartment stands out for its bright living room with kitchenette, characterized by large windows that open onto a spacious terrace. Here you can enjoy pleasant moments of relaxation surrounded by greenery, sipping a drink at sunset overlooking the pool.

The sleeping area consists of two well-lit double bedrooms and two finely finished bathrooms designed to ensure maximum comfort and privacy.

The property is completed by a large private solarium area, ideal for enjoying summer days in complete tranquility and by a double parking space in a secure garage available separately.

The Palms Edition residence not only offers unique architectural style but also exclusive services, including a beautiful swimming pool measuring 90 meters.

This property represents a rare opportunity in one of the most sought-after areas of Jesolo Lido, just steps from the sea and golden beach, with convenient access to all major services, shops, and restaurants in the area.
