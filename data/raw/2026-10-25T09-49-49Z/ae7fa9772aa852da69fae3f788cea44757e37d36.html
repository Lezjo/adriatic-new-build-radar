Overview

From October 1st to April 30th, within a refined new residential complex, we offer elegant apartments designed to provide you with maximum flexibility and comfort during your work or winter stay months.

To meet every need for space and privacy, the structure offers various types of apartments, from cozy one-bedroom solutions to spacious two-bedroom apartments available with either one or two bathrooms.

This proposal stands out for its exclusive all-inclusive formula that eliminates any management concerns: the monthly rent already includes all utilities and a true hotel-style service, including periodic changes of bed linens, towels, cleaning services, and high-speed Wi-Fi connection. To complete your daily comfort, you will find a convenient reserved parking space and concierge service always at your disposal.

For those looking to enrich their experience further, the complex also offers access to optional premium services such as an on-site laundry facility, an exclusive SPA area for relaxation moments, and a fine award-winning restaurant-pizzeria located right within the property.

- Rental prices starting from €1350/month (VAT included).

There are multiple housing options available and spaces are limited for the winter period.
