Overview

Welcome to your new home in Caorle!

Imagine a house where the sea is just a stroll away, where greenery accompanies every moment of the day, and where relaxation begins as soon as you cross the threshold. In Lido Altanea, one of the most sought-after areas of Caorle, a new residence with a pool is emerging, nestled in a tranquil and well-kept environment, only 200 meters from the beach.

The project is currently at an advanced rough stage with delivery expected by the end of 2027, offering elegant newly built three-room apartments designed for those looking for a modern, efficient, and comfortable seaside home.

Ground floor apartments feature a bright living area with living room and kitchenette, covered porch, and private garden—ideal spaces to fully enjoy the beautiful season and create moments to share with family. The sleeping area consists of two bedrooms: one master bedroom and one double bedroom—with options available for either one or two bathrooms based on individual needs.

First-floor units are distinguished by their spacious habitable terraces—perfect for outdoor dining, relaxing moments, and enjoyable summer evenings.

Each apartment will be constructed according to energy class A4 standards featuring photovoltaic systems along with innovative technologies that emphasize residential comfort while minimizing consumption. Each solution includes its own covered parking space.

To make your new home even more personal we collaborate with an interior design specialist who will assist buyers in selecting custom solutions.

The construction site is already open for visits by appointment: an opportunity to see the project up close and choose the option that best suits your needs.

Contact us for availability information, floor plans and specifications.
