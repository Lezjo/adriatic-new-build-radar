Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo Lido!

Just 50 meters from the beach, in the heart of Jesolo, in the renowned Piazza Aurora area, this exclusive newly built apartment on the fifth floor offers a stunning panoramic sea view and spaces designed for those who wish to live every day surrounded by comfort, elegance, and brightness.

The spacious interiors, totaling 160 square meters of commercial space, welcome a refined living area with a living room and kitchenette, made even brighter by direct access to the 36 square meter panoramic terrace—ideal for outdoor dining, relaxing moments, and summer evenings with the sea as a backdrop. The three terraces create continuity between indoor and outdoor spaces, fostering an airy atmosphere filled with natural light throughout the home.

The sleeping area features three spacious bedrooms and two modern elegant bathrooms within a residence where every detail has been crafted to offer maximum living comfort. The property is equipped with underfloor heating and energy class A4 specifications that ensure high energy efficiency and well-being in every season.

As it is a newly constructed building, floors, coverings, walls, and finishes can be completed and customized according to the tastes and needs of clients—thus offering the possibility to create truly tailored housing solutions.

Each unit also includes two covered parking spaces on the ground floor—a key added value in one of Jesolo's most sought-after areas.

A rare solution in Jesolo’s real estate landscape that combines strategic location with large spaces while allowing you to experience seaside living every day.
