Overview

San Stino di Livenza - Venice

In San Stino di Livenza, we offer this completely renovated duplex house with a private yard equipped with all comforts.

The property is developed over two floors and features modern finishes that enhance the brightness of the spaces. On the ground floor, you will find an open space living-kitchen area of over 50 sqm with direct access to the private garden, perfect for enjoying outdoor moments during beautiful summer days.

On the first floor, there are three bedrooms: one master bedroom, one double room, and one single room, each designed to ensure privacy and tranquility; the contemporary design makes every room unique and welcoming. Completing this floor is a modern bathroom with a shower cubicle.

In the basement, there is a bathroom with a laundry corner and a garage of over 30 sqm that can be used as a tavern.

The property is sold fully renovated with:

- ceiling heating and cooling,

- controlled mechanical ventilation system (VMC),

- external insulation of 14 cm,

- photovoltaic system of 6kw,

- storage batteries of 10kw,

- PVC frames with triple glazing.

The property comes with simultaneous transfer of tax benefits related to building recovery interventions.
