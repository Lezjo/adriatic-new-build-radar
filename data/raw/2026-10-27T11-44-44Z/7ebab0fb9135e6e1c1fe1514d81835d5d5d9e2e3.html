Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo Lido!

Located in an extremely quiet area, the apartment is just 150 meters from the sea, set within a building of only 9 residential units: an intimate and well-maintained context that guarantees privacy and discretion.

The property underwent a complete renovation in 2024, which included all systems, ensuring a modern and efficient home. The external aesthetics of the building will also be impeccable: the price includes facade renovations, providing added value that ensures a smooth purchase without unexpected costs.

Situated on the first floor, this apartment offers a comfortable and functional solution for those who want to avoid stairs or elevators. The interior spaces are well-distributed and ready to be lived in: the living area is functionally divided with separate kitchen and living room; the latter opens directly onto the balcony, ideal for enjoying the local climate. The sleeping area includes a spacious double bedroom.

Completing this property is one of its great strengths - a communal garden, a valuable and relaxing green space.

An intelligent investment:

Whether you are looking for a family home or an income-generating property, this residence represents an excellent opportunity. Its strategic proximity to the sea, small number of units, and total renovation make it highly attractive.

A linear house that is well-kept and ready to become your next seaside retreat.
