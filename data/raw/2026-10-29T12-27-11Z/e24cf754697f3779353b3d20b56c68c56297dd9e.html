Overview

Welcome to your new home in Caorle!

Just a few steps from the beach of Lido Altanea, within an exclusive newly built residence with a swimming pool, we offer for sale an elegant ground floor three-room apartment designed to provide comfort, functionality, and pleasant outdoor living spaces.

The property is developed entirely on one level and consists of a bright living area with a lounge and kitchenette, a master bedroom, a double room, and a windowed bathroom complete with shower box.

Outside, a large covered porch allows you to dine al fresco overlooking the private garden—an ideal space for relaxing in total tranquility or spending enjoyable moments with family and friends. The residence will be located in one of the most sought-after areas of Caorle, surrounded by greenery and just 200 meters from the sea. The complex will feature a beautiful communal swimming pool and offer a modern, private environment that is particularly well-maintained.

Construction is currently underway with completion expected by the end of 2027. Each apartment will be built to energy class A4 standards featuring photovoltaic systems, state-of-the-art plant solutions while paying particular attention to energy efficiency and residential well-being.

The property also includes an underground parking space. For those wishing for a fully customized home, there is an interior design consultancy service available thanks to collaboration with specialized professionals who will assist buyers in choosing finishes and furnishings. The construction site can already be visited by appointment.

Contact us for more information regarding plans, specifications, availability of remaining units.
