Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo Paese. Inside a modern duplex, part of the new and exclusive Glicine project, designed to offer comfort, quality, and maximum living independence.

We propose for sale an elegant solution developed over two levels, ground floor and first floor, ideal for those who desire generous and well-distributed spaces suitable for year-round living.

The property features:

- A spacious living room with kitchenette of over 50 sqm, bright and welcoming, perfect for daily life and entertaining guests.

- Hallway and technical room for practical and orderly space management.

- Two bedrooms, ideal for family or guests.

- Double bathroom to ensure maximum comfort.

The sleeping area is completed by a large attic space that can be customized according to personal needs (office, relaxation area or walk-in closet).

A distinctive element of the property is its outdoor spaces:

- Exclusive garden of considerable size, perfect for enjoying moments outdoors in complete privacy.

- Terraces on the first floor, ideal for relaxing and enjoying the tranquility of the surroundings.

The Glicine project has been designed to guarantee independence and privacy—fundamental characteristics of a modern duplex where each unit enjoys its own defined spaces.
