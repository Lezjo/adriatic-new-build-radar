Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo Lido! Just a few steps from the sea, in the heart of Jesolo and only 300 meters from the beach, this refined recently renovated apartment is the ideal solution for those who wish to live by the sea all year round without sacrificing comfort, privacy, and shared spaces.

Set within a well-maintained residence with a large communal garden, the property spans two levels offering bright and well-distributed environments. The first floor opens up to a spacious living area with an open kitchen, perfectly organized between dining area and lounge designed for moments of conviviality and relaxation. The habitable terrace with fireplace becomes the perfect spot for outdoor dinners and summer evenings together. Completing this level are a single bedroom with private terrace and a windowed bathroom with shower.

Ascending to the second level you are welcomed by a beautiful master bedroom, another windowed bathroom with shower, and a large solarium terrace—an exclusive space where you can relax in total tranquility under the sun.

Thanks to heating via radiators, this apartment is perfectly livable in every season transforming it into not just a holiday home but truly somewhere to live all year round.

The property also includes a convenient cellar and two parking spaces available separately if desired: €22,000 for both parking spaces and €12,000 for the cellar.

A solution that combines seaside atmosphere, modern space comfort, and practicality of strategic location near Piazza Milano and all main services.
