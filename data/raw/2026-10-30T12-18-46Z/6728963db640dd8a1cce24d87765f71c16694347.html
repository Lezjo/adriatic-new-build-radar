Overview

Welcome to your new home in Noventa di Piave!

In Romanziol, a quiet and sought-after fraction of Noventa di Piave, a new triplex is taking shape with a modern design, designed for those who seek comfort, independence, and quality living in a green and private setting.

The homes are spread over two levels and are designed to offer bright, functional spaces that are perfectly distributed. On the ground floor, we find a spacious living area with an open space kitchen and living room, a service bathroom, a garage directly connected to the house, and a pleasant external pergola ideal for outdoor dining and moments of relaxation.

The first floor hosts the sleeping area consisting of three bedrooms, a second bathroom, and two terraces that provide brightness and outdoor escape for the rooms.

Each unit also has its own exclusive private garden, perfect for families or anyone who loves enjoying outdoor spaces in total privacy.

Great attention has been given to energy efficiency and residential comfort: the houses will be built in energy class A4 and equipped with photovoltaic systems of 2.25 kW per unit, ensuring high energy savings and sustainability.

Delivery is scheduled for September 2026.

Only two units remain available: an exclusive opportunity to live in a new modern residence that is elegant yet immersed in the tranquility of Romanziol.

Experience peace—finally at your own home!
