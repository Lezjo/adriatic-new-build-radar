Overview

OLMI DI SAN BIAGIO (TV)

In a quiet residential area of Olmi, a fraction of the municipality of San Biagio di Callalta, we offer two newly built four-room apartments scheduled for completion in 2026 with energy class A4.

Located just 100 meters from essential services such as supermarkets, schools, and public transport, this property represents the ideal solution for families or young couples seeking modern comfort without sacrificing daily convenience.

The apartments are situated on the first floor within an elegant setting consisting of only five housing units, ensuring privacy and tranquility.

The bright living areas of approximately 40 sqm open onto a habitable terrace of 22 sqm where you can enjoy beautiful days outdoors.

Completing the property are three spacious bedrooms, two modern bathrooms equipped with all necessary comforts, and a storage room. One apartment also features a walk-in closet.

Additionally, the private garage measuring about 17 sqm and two reserved parking spaces ensure practicality when it comes to parking.

The strategic location of the property allows easy connections to nearby cities such as Treviso and Conegliano, making it perfect for those who work close by but wish to live away from urban chaos.
