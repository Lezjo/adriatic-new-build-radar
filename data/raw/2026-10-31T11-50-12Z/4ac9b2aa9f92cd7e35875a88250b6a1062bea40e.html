Overview

We offer for sale an interesting plot of land with high potential for enhancement, ideal for the realization of an important project in line with the planned urban development projects.

The property is suitable for multiple uses, representing a unique opportunity for investors, businesses, and developers seeking a strategic area with strong tourist-commercial and nautical vocation.

Among the main development possibilities:

- Construction of a panoramic restaurant with terraces overlooking the water.

- Bar, lounge, venues, and entertainment activities.

- Marina with docks for boat mooring.

- Storage facilities with services dedicated to boating.

- Mixed-use tourist-commercial complex as outlined in the attached projects.

The area enjoys a particularly strategic position and has a depth reaching up to approximately 8 meters, allowing access and mooring even for larger vessels, making it especially attractive for initiatives related to the nautical sector.

This is a rare opportunity to develop a high-value-added project in an area of strong tourist and economic interest.
