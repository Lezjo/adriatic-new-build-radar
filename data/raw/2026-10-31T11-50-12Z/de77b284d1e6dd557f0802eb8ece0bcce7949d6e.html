Overview

PORTOGRUARO - (Venice) Discover a unique investment opportunity with this rustic ruin located in the heart of the picturesque area of Lugugnana, within the municipality of Portogruaro. With a total surface area of 400 sqm, this property offers endless possibilities for your creativity and vision.

The rustic structure presents itself as an intriguing renovation project, ideal for those wishing to create their dream home or develop a hospitality business immersed in the tranquility of the Venetian countryside. The ample space available allows for various architectural solutions and customizations according to your tastes.

The property is already divided into two distinct units; there is no possibility for demolition and reconstruction, expansion or elevation, nor further subdivision. However, besides restoration, there are still 510 cubic meters of land usable for a new residential building.

The strategic location of the property allows you to enjoy the natural beauty of the surrounding hills while being close to essential services and amenities offered by the vibrant city of Portogruaro. Here you will find shops, traditional restaurants, and cultural events that enrich local experiences.

Additionally, the territory is characterized by beautiful rural landscapes and historic sites that make every walk truly enjoyable. Proximity to other tourist locations makes this place not only perfect for living but also as a base from which to explore Veneto's wonders.

Don't miss out on transforming this rustic into an exclusive residence or high-potential accommodation! Contact our agency immediately for more information or to schedule an on-site visit; we will be happy to assist you step by step in realizing your real estate project.
