Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo Lido!

This exclusive four-room apartment of new construction is located on the fifth floor of the prestigious W-ONE residence, in the heart of Jesolo Lido. Characterized by maximum efficiency with Energy Class A4, the property (Unit U19) offers spacious areas ideal for families or those seeking maximum comfort, distributed in a bright open-space living area, three cozy bedrooms, two carefully finished bathrooms, and a beautiful livable terrace. The property also includes a private parking space and a reserved beach spot.

- The Spaces of the Four-Room Apartment and Livable Terrace

The living area stands out for its extraordinary brightness and harmoniously blends into the large livable terrace, creating an ideal environment for outdoor dinners and moments of pure relaxation enhanced by being on the fifth floor. The sleeping area has been meticulously designed to offer comfort and privacy, consisting of three comfortable bedrooms and two contemporary-designed bathrooms tailored to meet daily needs.

- Exclusive Services of the Residence

Living in W-ONE means accessing an experience of superior comfort. Outside there is a large communal pool perfect for summer days while inside there is a fully equipped gym accessible 24/7. The private parking space and reserved beach spot ensure you enjoy summer season with total privacy and convenience just steps from home.

- Benefits and Purchase Services

The investment becomes even more advantageous thanks to the Earthquake Bonus Purchases program; therefore, the proposed price has already been calculated net of tax benefits. For those who desire a customized solution, our interior designers are available to define materials and finishes that personalize your home according to your style. Furthermore, if you need to sell your current property we manage all aspects related to it ensuring that transitioning into your new home is simple and worry-free.

Live by the sea—finally at home.
