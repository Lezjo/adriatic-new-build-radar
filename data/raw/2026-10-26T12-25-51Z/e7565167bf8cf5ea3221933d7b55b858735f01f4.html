DELIVERY DECEMBER 2027 - NEW EXCLUSIVE RESIDENCE OF ONLY 6 HOUSING UNITS

HOME 5.1 - is born in one of the most interesting and rapidly evolving areas of the territory, a contemporary residential project designed for those who wish to live in a modern, efficient home that is destined to maintain its value over time. An exclusive residence with only 6 units, including two-bedroom and three-bedroom apartments, designed to offer quality living, daily comfort, and superior construction performance. Clean architectural lines, bright environments, private outdoor spaces, and innovative building technologies give rise to a new concept of living: more evolved, more sustainable, more intelligent.

HOME 5.1 LOOKS TO THE FUTURE!

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES:

- HEAVY STEEL STRUCTURE

HOME 5.1 is built with high-performance heavy steel structure; an advanced construction solution that ensures high solidity, long durability and important seismic performance. A modern technology that offers construction precision, reliability over time and concrete structural value: unlike traditional constructions, steel maintains its value as raw material over time.

- NZEB CLASS: NEARLY ZERO ENERGY BUILDING

The project is developed according to NZEB (Nearly Zero Energy Building) standards to guarantee reduced consumption levels ,high energy efficiency ,and maximum housing comfort . Advanced thermal insulation ,acoustic insulation technologies ,and always comfortable environments in every season allow you to live sustainably and intelligently.

- PRIVATE OUTDOOR SPACES

Each apartment has terraces and private outdoor spaces conceived as a natural extension of the living area . Ideal settings for creating relaxation areas ,installing bioclimatic pergolas,and enjoying moments outdoors with total privacy and independence.

- GUARANTEED ACCESSIBILITY

HOME 5.1 is designed to be accessible and functional at every stage of life . The modern elevator eliminates architectural barriers ensuring convenience practicality,and freedom of movement for all.

includ e two-bedroom and three-bedroom apartments characterized by modern bright living areas served by elevators complete with terraces private outdoor spaces dedicated parking spots . The project offers customizable finishes elevated thermal acoustic comfort high energy efficiency guaranteeing perfect blend between DESIGN COMFORT AND FUNCTIONALITY .

Every environment has been studied to provide a sense of balance brightness everyday well-being .
