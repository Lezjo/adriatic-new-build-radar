Overview

Welcome to your new home in Caorle!

Just a few steps from the beach of Lido Altanea, a new concept of seaside living comes to life. This modern residence, set in a tranquil and private environment less than 200 meters from the beach, is designed for those seeking the perfect balance between comfort, nature, and total relaxation.

The project, currently under construction with delivery expected by the end of 2027, offers refined newly built apartments: the perfect retreat for anyone dreaming of an elegant seaside residence that is efficient and crafted to provide moments of pure well-being.

This ground floor apartment stands out for its privilege of double private exposure and complete continuity between indoor and outdoor spaces.

The heart of the home is the bright open-plan living area that opens directly onto a spacious covered terrace: a true outdoor lounge where you can enjoy moments of pure relaxation at any time of day. A hallway leads to the sleeping area, which features a modern functional bathroom and a large master bedroom that overlooks its own private back garden: an intimate and quiet oasis where you can wake up each morning immersed in tranquility.

Completing the amenities of the residence is a magnificent pool exclusively for residents. Each unit is designed to ensure maximum energy savings and high living comfort. Additionally, to make this two-room apartment truly yours, we collaborate with specialists in interior design who will guide you step-by-step through choosing tailored solutions and furnishings.

The construction site is already visitable by appointment: a unique opportunity to see the project up close and start imagining your new life by the sea. Block 'C' is currently awaiting approval.

Contact us for availability information, floor plans, and specifications.

Эта квартира расположена на первом этаже и выделяется привилегией двойного частного выхода и полной непрерывностью между внутренними и внешними пространствами.
