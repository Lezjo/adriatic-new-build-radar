Overview

Navolè di Gorgo al Monticano (Treviso)

If you are a lover of the countryside and seeking tranquility, don't miss this extraordinary opportunity! Just 5 minutes from the main services of Mansuè and Gorgo al Monticano, we offer a rustic property immersed in greenery with almost 4000 sqm of land. The building develops over two above-ground levels with a total area of approximately 160 sqm. The house is accompanied by storage annexes and a historic former barn measuring about 480 sqm, offering endless possibilities: from creating additional living spaces to establishing activities such as agritourism, B&Bs, educational farms or similar ventures. Both buildings have undergone roof renovations including waterproofing and gutters.

The strategic location of the property allows for easy connections to nearby cities while maintaining the intimacy typical of rural life.
