A RARE AND UNFINDABLE OPPORTUNITY IN JESOLO!

-- Year of Construction 2021 | Sale Subject to Registration Tax, NO VAT! --

The apartment is sold FULLY furnished with prestigious and high-level furniture, meticulously crafted down to the smallest details and ready for immediate living!

Located on the third floor of an elegant building with only eight residential units served by an elevator, the property is just 200 meters from the beach, near Piazza Milano: a pleasantly quiet area but with all comforts, venues, and services at your fingertips.

Crossing the threshold, we are welcomed into an extraordinarily bright and spacious living area. From here, the environment opens naturally to the outside thanks to a beautiful livable terrace: a perfect space for outdoor dining, sipping an aperitif at sunset or simply relaxing while admiring the glimpse and view of the sea on the horizon.

To ensure privacy and silence there’s a hallway with a sliding door that leads to the sleeping area. The spaces include a spacious master bedroom, an extremely versatile second double room, and a modern bathroom with quality finishes.

Completing this unique property is an essential private parking space—an added value fundamental for forgetting about your car and enjoying the sea in total serenity.
