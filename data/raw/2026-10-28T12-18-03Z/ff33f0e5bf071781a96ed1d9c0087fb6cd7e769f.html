Overview

Welcome to your new home in Jesolo!

Located just 150 meters from the beach, in the vibrant heart of the city and a stone's throw from Piazza Drago. Ascending to the sixth floor, you are greeted by a residence characterized by generous spaces and an internal layout designed to offer maximum comfort and livability.

The property is delivered free and partially unfurnished, representing a perfect opportunity for those looking to create their dream seaside home or make a high-profile investment.

Crossing the threshold, you enter a bright living room with kitchenette, a comfortable environment that naturally extends outdoors thanks to nearly 30 square meters of covered and uncovered space between loggia and terrace: a true outdoor living area where one can enjoy moments together, set up a lounge zone or create an enchanting winter garden.

What truly makes this property extraordinary is its division of sleeping areas, designed to ensure complete independence. On one side of the living area lies a private master suite consisting of a spacious double bedroom with hallway and dedicated windowed bathroom. On the opposite side of the day zone, completely separate and independent, there are two additional bedrooms served by another bathroom and another private terrace.

This clear separation between the master wing and the rest of the house provides invaluable privacy, ideal for welcoming family as well as hosting friends in utmost tranquility.

Completing this offering is an assigned parking space.

The privilege of ample spaces, the scent of sea air just steps away, and uncompromised privacy; contact us now to turn your idea of seaside living into reality!
